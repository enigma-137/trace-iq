# TraceIQ Vision

TraceIQ helps product teams understand what users do, where they drop off, why conversions fail, which journeys lead to retention, and how critical flows perform.

## Target Customers

- Fintechs tracking onboarding, KYC, payments, and compliance-sensitive journeys.
- Edtechs measuring course discovery, enrollment, activation, and retention.
- Food delivery platforms monitoring ordering, checkout, delivery, and refund flows.
- SaaS products diagnosing trials, activation, feature adoption, and expansion paths.
- Growth-stage startups that need product analytics before building an internal data platform.

## Product Pillars

- Event tracking as the durable behavioral source of truth.
- Funnel analytics for conversion-critical journeys.
- User timelines that make individual behavior explainable.
- Session replay for qualitative diagnosis.
- Conversion intelligence that converts raw behavior into recommended actions.
- AI-powered insights that surface anomalies and bottlenecks.
