The TraceIQ MCP server now runs remotely at `api.traceiq.run/mcp` with OAuth sign-in. Add it as a connector in Claude, ChatGPT or Codex, sign in, and your assistant can query funnels, errors and replays with no local setup.

- Trends, funnel results, experiment results and project health draw inline charts in clients that support MCP Apps.
- Resources, prompts and "Open in TraceIQ" links come with every result, and deletes or flag changes ask you to confirm first.
