Ask Claude, Cursor or Codex to create a flag, roll it out to 10% of Pro users, or change who it targets. Two new MCP tools, `create_feature_flag` and `update_feature_flag`, cover the key, rollout percentage, environment and targeting rules. New flags start off, and any change to who sees a flag asks you to confirm first.

- Funnel steps created from MCP can have readable labels.
- **Fixed:** renaming a funnel, or changing only its steps, from MCP failed. Both work now.
