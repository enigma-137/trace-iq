Give your users one place to check whether your product is up. Each project can publish a status page at `traceiq.run/status/your-name`, free on every plan.

- Pick which synthetic monitors to show and give them public names. Their state and 90 days of uptime come from real monitor runs.
- Report incidents and post updates as you go, from Investigating to Resolved, and schedule maintenance that starts and ends on its own.
- Only the names and text you write are public. Monitor URLs, failure details and screenshots never appear on the page.

Set it up under Observability → Status page.
