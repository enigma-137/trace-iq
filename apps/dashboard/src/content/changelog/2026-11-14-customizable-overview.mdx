Choose the cards on your Overview and arrange them the way you read them. Build a custom card from any event, as a count or unique people, or request a card you'd like us to add.

- Today's Story weighs failures by how serious they are, so a normal day with a few expired sessions no longer reads like an incident, and a recovered outage isn't treated as ongoing.
- Locations count visitors rather than events, so one very active user no longer makes their country lead.
